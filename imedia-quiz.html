EXAMINER TIP:

Mood Board

R093 TA3

Purpose: Generates visual ideas and conveys aesthetic feel, tone, and vibe to the client.

Contents: Sample photos, textures, materials, colour palettes, font samples, brief annotations.

Examiner Rule: A Mood Board defines feeling/atmosphere, NEVER layout or measurements!

Visualisation Diagram

R093 TA3

Purpose: Plans static spatial composition and proportions of a single non-moving product.

Contents: Layout positioning, logo placement, headline sizing, dimensions, colour codes, margins.

Use for: Posters, magazine covers, game packaging, web page wireframes.

Storyboard

R093 TA3

Purpose: Maps sequential chronological flow of moving or time-based media.

Contents: Illustrated panels, shot types (CU, MS, LS), camera angles, movement, sound/dialogue, timings.

Use for: Video adverts, films, game cutscenes, animations.

Script

R093 TA3

Purpose: Written directions and spoken lines for actors, voice talent, and crew.

Contents: Sluglines (INT/EXT - DAY/NIGHT), centered character names, dialogue, parentheticals, SFX.

Key Convention: Standard screenplay format written in the active present tense.
ECU

Extreme Close-Up

Focuses purely on eyes, mouth, or small object. Conveys intense emotion, tension, or subtle detail.

CU

Close-Up

Head and shoulders. Emphasizes character facial expressions and viewer empathy.

MS

Mid Shot

Waist-up. Natural conversation distance showing body language and nearby environment.

LS

Long Shot

Full body from head to toe. Places the character clearly inside their physical setting.

HA

High Angle

Camera looks down on subject. Makes character appear weak, vulnerable, or insignificant.

LA

Low Angle

Camera looks up at subject. Makes character appear dominant, heroic, or intimidating.
